# 05 — Audience Profile

## ICP (Ideal Customer Profile)

- **Company size.** 5M–100M ARR.
- **Function we sell to.** RevOps, Customer Operations, or the founder/COO.
- **Existing AI footprint.** At least one AI tool already in production; at least one shelved pilot.
- **Buying authority.** The contact can authorize $10k–$50k/yr without committee.
- **Geography.** US/EU/AU first; we'll take APAC if the contact is bilingual.
- **Sector.** SaaS, marketplaces, B2B services. Not regulated finance for v1.

## Primary persona — Mira (VP RevOps, 7M ARR SaaS)

- 34, three years into the role. Reports to the CRO.
- Has run two AI pilots in the past 12 months — one ICP-scoring tool that shipped, one inbound triage tool that stalled.
- Uses Notion, HubSpot, Slack, Salesloft, Apollo, and Looker daily.
- Lives in Slack DMs, AdvisorTo Slack groups (RevGenius, Pavilion), and curated newsletters (Lenny, RevOps Co-op).
- **Goals.** Hit her pipeline number with one fewer SDR. Get a Friday afternoon back.
- **Frustrations.** Pilots that need 6 weeks of integration before the first email goes out. Sales decks that don't list a price.
- **Buying trigger.** A peer ships an agent that books eight meetings in week one.

## Secondary persona — Alex (Agency principal, 14-person shop)

- 41, runs a B2B marketing/RevOps agency in NYC.
- Resells productized services to ten clients.
- Already pays for OpenAI, Anthropic, and Apollo on his own card.
- **Goals.** Add agent labor to his retainer without hiring two more humans.
- **Frustrations.** Cannot stand behind a tool with no provenance — one bad demo and a client churns.
- **Buying trigger.** A vetted catalog he can put a margin on.

## Anti-personas (we don't sell to)

- **The no-code dabbler.** Wants a playground. We don't want to compete on tinkerability.
- **The Fortune-500 procurement office.** Wants a 60-page MSA. Wave 2 is not staffed for it.
- **The pure consumer.** No personal AI agent fits our offer.
