# 03 — User Journeys

Three journeys, all written from the buyer's point of view. Time targets are real targets, not aspirations.

## Journey 1 — Discovery (60 seconds)

**Trigger.** Mira opens the landing from a peer's Slack DM. She has never heard of us.

1. Hero loads. She sees three stacked AgentCards on the left and a "Concierge — online" rail on the right that has already typed the first message.
2. She reads four lot ribbons under the hero — "Lot 042 · Anders, SDR — eighteen ops teams since Feb." Numbers, names, no copy that smells like marketing.
3. She scrolls. Catalog grid appears: 6 cards, each with a 4-row provenance table.
4. She is convinced this is "a real shelf, not a marketplace" within 60 seconds. She clicks **Demo** on the SDR card.

**Success metric.** 60-second time-to-CTA, zero confusion about category.

## Journey 2 — First value (under 10 minutes)

**Trigger.** Mira clicked **Demo** on the Sales SDR.

1. Demo opens an in-page sheet. The Concierge rail asks: "Want to see Anders work your inbox or ours? — Yours / Theirs."
2. She picks "Theirs" because it's faster. The Concierge runs a 4-step scripted demo: pull a real-looking lead, draft a first-touch email, qualify against ICP, hand off to her with a calendar slot.
3. At step 4, the Concierge surfaces three tiers: Trial / Pro / Enterprise. Each has a NOWPayments crypto CTA.
4. Mira clicks **Buy — Pro**. A server-side `POST /v1/invoice` returns a NOWPayments hosted invoice URL. She is redirected.
5. She closes the tab. She has not paid yet — but she has seen the agent work end-to-end and she knows the price. Total elapsed: 8 minutes.

**Success metric.** First-value (saw the agent run AND saw a price) under 10 minutes.

## Journey 3 — Recurring use (week two)

**Trigger.** Mira completed payment last week. The agent has been running.

1. She gets a weekly digest (delivered via the future app tier — stubbed for v1) — "Anders sent 312 first-touch emails, 41 replies, 8 meetings booked. Two flagged for human."
2. She clicks the digest. The dashboard (apps/app/, future) shows Anders's last seven days against her ICP and provenance footer.
3. She decides to add a second agent — Lot 047, the support concierge. The catalog opens with a "Owned" filter showing what she already has. She buys the second agent through the same NOWPayments path.
4. Friction is the same as week one: one click to demo, one click to buy.

**Success metric.** Adding the second agent takes under 5 minutes. StampedAgents footer is consistent across both purchases.
