# 09 — Go-to-Market (90-day plan)

A weekly rhythm. Each week's milestone is a single, falsifiable outcome — not a list of activities.

## Week 1 — Catalog goes live

- **Mon.** Landing deployed at `agent-that-sells-agents.prin7r.com`. Six lots visible. NOWPayments invoice tested live.
- **Wed.** Lot 042 (Anders, SDR) named operator (Mira) on the page.
- **Fri.** First Concierge transcript captured (internal demo against a Prin7r prospect).
- **Done = catalog live + at least one Concierge demo recorded.**

## Week 2 — First three referrals

- DM 30 named operators (peer Slack DMs, no copy/paste). Direct link to a single lot.
- **Done = three booked Concierge demos.**

## Week 3 — First paying customer

- The first three demos turn into one Trial. NOWPayments invoice paid. Internal celebration: send the lot number, not balloons.
- **Done = one paid Trial.**

## Week 4 — Lot 047 (Support concierge) launched

- Same provenance template, second domain (support).
- **Done = two lots paid for, two named operators on the catalog.**

## Weeks 5–8 — Ten Trials, three Pros

- Distribution: weekly lot drop in Pavilion + RevOps Co-op. Newsletter sponsorship #1.
- Objection-handling captured into the Concierge rail script.
- **Done at week 8 = $5k MRR.**

## Weeks 9–12 — First Enterprise contract

- Concierge-led close on a named account. Custom agent retrain. Annual invoice via NOWPayments.
- Agency partner program (Alex) goes live; partner code on each agent page.
- Public Eval Log #1 published.
- **Done at week 12 = $12k MRR + one Enterprise + Alex onboarded.**

## Launch sequence

- **Day -7.** Soft-launch: the catalog is live, but only peers in three Slack groups know.
- **Day 0.** Public launch: a single email to 300 operators. No PR, no Product Hunt, no LinkedIn announcement.
- **Day 7.** First Eval Log on the public docs site.
- **Day 21.** First lot drop + paid Trial story on the blog. No video.
- **Day 60.** Newsletter sponsorship #1 (Pavilion or Lenny).
- **Day 90.** Read the report. Either we doubled MRR or we kill three lots and concentrate.
